NotesMy

NotesMy is a comprehensive and efficient note-taking mobile application designed to help users organize their thoughts and ideas seamlessly. Developed using Java and SQLite for robust data management, the app offers a range of features including creating, editing, and deleting notes, searching through saved content, categorizing notes into folders for better organization, and syncing data across devices. With a clean, intuitive interface that emphasizes simplicity and speed, NotesMy ensures that users can quickly capture ideas on the go, access their notes anytime, and maintain a clutter-free digital workspace. The app prioritizes data persistence and security, making it a reliable tool for students, professionals, and anyone needing to keep track of important information.

TicTacToe

The Tic Tac Toe Game is based on a two-player game. Each player chooses between X and O. The Player plays one move at a time simultaneously. In a move, a player can choose any position from a 3x3 grid. The goal here is to get three consecutive X or O in a horizontal, vertical, or diagonal direction. There will be a single activity in this application. This activity will show a 3x3 grid. The status of the game will be displayed at the bottom.
